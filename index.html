About Us

Storytelling with an artistic eye

Motion Media Studios is a Christian-owned video production company based in So-Cal. We take an innovative approach to visual storytelling, ensuring that every project is developed in an artistic manner.

We provide all types of video production services — producing original video content for every client, from corporate video production to capturing special moments in everyday events.

Motion Media Studios is your one-stop-shop for casual and professional event services, offering affordable, budget-friendly packages for weddings, birthdays, quinceañeras, anniversaries, and much, much more.
